Seema aur privacy

ID ka matlab naam/identity nahi. Ek session me box ko trace karta hai. Lambi der baad lautne par same ID ki guarantee nahi. Occlusion, bheed, similar kapde ya camera movement par ID badal ya galat match ho sakti hai.

Person label se bachcha/bada pakka nahi hota: age unknown. Car ka exact make/model unknown. Bus/car/truck bhi kabhi galat ho sakte hain. Far scan missing pixels ko wapas nahi laata. Smooth boxes detections ke beech short motion prediction hain, nayi detection nahi.

Camera/video upload nahi hota. Frames, clothing colour aur IDs RAM me rehte hain; stop/page band hone par clear. Reset sirf IDs clear karta hai. Koi face recognition, recording, theft, helmet ya speed feature nahi.

Camera ke liye HTTPS aur permission chahiye. Bad light/blur/small objects miss ho sakte hain. Test demo hai, safety ya policing decisions ke liye nahi.
